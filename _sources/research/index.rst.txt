.. card:: KEOR Family Distribution Research

  .. grid:: 2

    .. grid-item::
      :columns: 12 4 4 4

      .. image:: images/project2.png
        :alt: Project 2
        :width: 30%

    .. grid-item::
      :columns: 12 8 8 8

      Explores the **Kumaraswamy Exponentiated Odds Ratio (KEOR) family** for
      lifetime data. Unifies and extends known models with additional shape
      control for skewness and tails, yielding stronger fits and clear
      reliability/biomedical interpretations. :doc:`More details<project2>`
