Kumaraswamy Exponentiated Odds Ratio Family: Mathematical Insights and Practical Applications
======================================================================================================

.. card:: Story

  We developed the KEOR family to unify and extend lifetime models with extra
  shape control for skewness and tails. I contributed by running simulations
  and checking diagnostics as we compared fits and clarified when KEOR brings
  clearer interpretation than classic baselines. The process emphasized
  readable models, careful validation, and practical gains over exhaustive
  complexity.
