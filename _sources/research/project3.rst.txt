Data-driven Analysis of Chemical Short-range Order-Property Correlations in Multi-principal Element Alloys
==============================================================================================================

.. card:: Story

  We investigated how chemical short-range order (SRO) relates to material
  properties in multi-principal element alloys using a clear, data-driven
  pipeline. We built SRO descriptors, compared regularized baselines, and
  focused on interpretability and reproducibility. A small set of descriptors
  consistently explained most variation, providing a practical baseline for
  future modeling.
